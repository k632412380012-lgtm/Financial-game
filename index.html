      How to play — JPY Carry Trade

      4 rounds. Borrow yen at near-zero rates, hold risk assets funded by the spread, and survive the unwind.
      1

      Each round opens with market news and BOJ / FX data. Paying for a reveal shows how reliable one story is.

      2

      Review any open JPY loans — principal and accrued interest are tracked separately per loan.

      3

      Sell assets or withdraw US Cash first if you need liquidity.

      4

      Repay a loan in full if you have the USD cash for it — no partial repayments.

      5

      Borrow JPY as a % of your role's cap, then buy US Tech, S&P 500, or deposit into US Cash.

      6

      After round 4, everything is auto-liquidated and all yen debt is settled at the final rate.
